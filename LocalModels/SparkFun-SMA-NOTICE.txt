Project adaptations: the model path uses KIPRJMOD, and the four ground pads
use solid zone connections. Pad geometry is unchanged from SparkFun.
The STEP model is unmodified. No endorsement by SparkFun is implied.

Ground-pad center spacing: 5.08 mm. All pads: 1.5 x 4 mm.
J1 is rotated -90 degrees at (138.619, 53) mm. The fabrication drawing's
body shoulder at local y = -2.381 mm aligns with the board edge x = 141 mm.
Pads end 0.381 mm inside that edge. Verify the actual connector against
a 1:1 print; its manufacturer and axial dimensions are not confirmed.
